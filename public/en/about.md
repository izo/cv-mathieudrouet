# About

## Product. Technology. AI. From field insight to shipped systems.

I work at the intersection of three responsibilities that are often separated: understanding the product problem, deciding the technology architecture, and determining where AI creates real value. My background spans technical leadership and product leadership, and today includes work as a **Chief AI, Technology & Product Officer (CATPO)**.

## What I actually do

I work on complex B2B products, often involving legacy systems, expert users and strong business constraints.

- **Product & business** — field discovery, strategy, roadmap, prioritisation and go-to-market.
- **Technology** — architecture, systems modernisation, APIs, infrastructure and technical trade-offs.
- **AI** — LLM architecture, agents, MCP, model selection and integration of AI capabilities into products.
- **Hands-on delivery** — I can move from strategic decisions to prototypes and shipped products without losing touch with the teams building them.

Since May 2026, I have worked freelance as **Chief AI, Technology & Product Officer at Lilabs**.

## AI-native delivery

I use AI-assisted development and agentic systems as a production layer, not a demo. I also built Ulk, a multi-agent framework designed to industrialise the idea → prototype → delivery cycle.

The goal is not to add AI everywhere. It is to choose the right model, level of autonomy and architecture for the product's value, cost, latency and constraints.

## Working principles

- No product management without contact with the field.
- No AI added simply to tick a roadmap box.
- Product, technology and AI decisions should be made together, not sequentially.

## What I'm looking for now

Contexts where Product, Technology and AI contribute directly to company strategy, with a short line to leadership and the ability to turn decisions into real products quickly.
